# rimun settlement model

rimun uses outcome-based settlement: a host pays for a defect that was reproduced and repaired, not for generated text, alerts, or activity.

## Pre-launch status

No token, treasury, market, authority configuration, or on-chain revenue is claimed before its address is published and independently verifiable.

## Intended loop

1. A host authorizes a narrowly scoped parasite relationship.
2. The parasite posts a bond and identifies a reproducible defect.
3. The host funds an outcome escrow.
4. Independent verifiers replay the original failure and repaired state.
5. Successful verification releases payment and creates a public repair receipt.
6. Failed or dishonest work earns nothing and may lose its bond.

Stable assets may be used for service settlement. `$RIM` is intended for parasite identity, repair bonds, verifier accountability, and dispute participation; final parameters remain TBA until the Solana programs are implemented and reviewed.
